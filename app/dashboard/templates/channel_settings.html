Help

                

                    
Remove keywords

                    
You can add one keyword at a time.

                    
Example:

                    
BREAKINGAdvertisementUrgent

                    
Matching is case-sensitive.
BREAKING, Breaking and breaking are treated as different words.

                

                

                    
Replace words

                    
Add rules to replace words or phrases.

                    
Example:

                    
Israel => OccupationUSA => United StatesBreaking => News

                    
Replacement is case-sensitive.
Israel and israel are treated as different words.

                

                

                    
Processing order

                    
  1. Remove keywords
  2. Replace words
  3. Translate, when enabled
  4. Add prefix and suffix

                

                

                    
Translation preview

                    
Preview uses the current form values without saving them. Turn translation on and choose two different languages to translate.

                    
The first request may take longer while the model loads. If a preview fails, your entries stay in place.
