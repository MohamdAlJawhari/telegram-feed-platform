Help

Remove keywords

You can write one keyword per line: 

            BREAKING
            Advertisement
            Urgent

or comma separated: 

            BREAKING,Advertisement,Urgent
        
Matching is case-sensitive.
            BREAKING, Breaking and breaking are treated as different words.

Replace words

Write each rule as: 

old=>new

Example: 

            Israel=>Occupation
            USA=>United States
            Breaking=>News

Comma-separated rules also work: 

Israel=>Occupation,USA=>United States
Replacement is case-sensitive.
            Israel and israel are treated as different words.

Processing order

  1. Remove keywords
  2. Replace words
  3. Add prefix
  4. Add suffix
